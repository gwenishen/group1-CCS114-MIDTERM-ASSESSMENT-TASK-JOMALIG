DESTINATION REVIEW

History & Unique Characteristics of Jomalig Island

Jomalig is an isolated island municipality situated in the outermost part of Quezon province. Often referred to as the ultimate remote frontier, it rewards intrepid travelers with complete peace and unspoiled natural beauty.

The island's standout feature is its distinctive golden-amber sand, formed over centuries by mineral weathering. Combined with expansive agoho forests and pristine waters, it offers a unique ecosystem unlike any other beach destination in the country.

Comprehensive Travel Guide

Everything you need to know about routes, estimated expenses, and packing checklists before embarking on your Jomalig journey.

How to Get to Jomalig Island

Take a van or bus from Legarda (Manila) or Cubao bound for Infanta or Real, Quezon (approx. 3 to 4 hours travel time). Disembark at the Real Port (Ungos Port).

Ride a passenger outrigger boat (locally known as "lantsa") from Ungos Port to Jomalig Island. The boat ride takes approximately 5 to 7 hours depending on sea and weather conditions.

Upon arrival at Jomalig Port, register at the Tourism Office to pay the environmental fee. Afterwards, hire a local habal-habal (motorcycle) driver to transport you directly to your chosen resort or homestay.

Estimated Budget Breakdown (Per Person)

Expense Item Estimated Cost (PHP) Notes
Bus / Van Fare (Manila to Real Port roundtrip) ₱800 - ₱1,000 Standard airconditioned van or bus
Boat Fare (Real Port to Jomalig roundtrip) ₱1,000 - ₱2,000 Includes passenger fee
Environmental & Tourism Fees ₱350 Paid at the port upon arrival
Homestay / Camping & Food (3 Days / 2 Nights) ₱2,500 - ₱4,000 Varies based on accommodation choice

Essential Packing Checklist

  • Cash (No major ATMs available on the island)
  • Dry bags / Waterproof phone cases
  • Power banks & solar chargers (Limited electricity)
  • Reef-safe sunscreen & insect repellent
  • Light tropical clothing & swimwear
  • Reusable water bottle & personal first aid kit

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Cellular signal (Smart/Globe) is available in primary areas, but internet connectivity can be spotty or weak depending on the specific location and weather conditions.

Accommodations range from basic beachfront resorts and homestays to designated tent camping grounds along Salibungot and nearby beaches.

The dry summer months from March to May are ideal for visiting Jomalig to experience calmer seas and optimal sunny beach weather.
